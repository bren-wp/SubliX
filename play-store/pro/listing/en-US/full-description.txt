Plan recurring expenses with SubliX Pro by BRENDIGO, an offline-first subscription manager with advanced tools for analyzing your own records.

SubliX Pro includes the essential subscription tracker plus deeper planning tools. No account, bank integration or cloud synchronization is required.

SUBSCRIPTION MANAGEMENT
• Add and organize subscriptions with weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly schedules
• See expected renewals and monthly equivalent costs
• Optional local renewal notifications and manual payment history
• Record trial dates, usage labels and provider management links
• Manually export or import JSON backups

ADVANCED PRO TOOLS
• Forecast expected renewal payments over 6, 12 or 24 months
• Create individual monthly budgets for spending categories
• Compare possible savings in a what-if cancellation scenario
• Filter and sort plans by due date, name and estimated cost
• Review subscription price changes you recorded
• Export subscription, payment history and forecast CSV reports

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Subscriptions, recorded payments and budgets stay on your Android device. There is no subscription-provider login, no bank password and no automatic cloud account.

IMPORTANT
Forecasts and savings are estimates derived only from your own entries. The app does not pay bills or cancel external services. CSV reports and JSON backups are currently unencrypted; choose private storage locations. Reminders are approximate and may be delayed.

SubliX Pro is a separate Android app with its own local database. To move records from another SubliX edition, export JSON there and import it here.
