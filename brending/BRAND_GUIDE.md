## Typography and behavior
Inter (web, fallback system), default Android Roboto; spacing base 8dp; touch targets >=48dp; min accessible text contrast; cards 16–24dp radius. Default dark theme, optional light and AMOLED. Scalable layouts for phone/tablet/foldable.

## Logo usage
Use `logo.svg` with a clear background and 16px breathing space. Free uses purple icon; Pro uses premium gold accent. Do not use gold to imply a financial guarantee. Signature on website footer, app about screen, README, and store assets: **by BRENDIGO**. The icon SVGs are original vector designs created for this repository.

## Screen references
`screens/01-home.svg` through `screens/20-about.svg` are **illustrative design concepts**, not screenshots of compiled application. Do not upload them as authentic product screenshots. Replace them with real Android emulator captures and later real-device tests before Play Store publication. The screenshot workflow commits authentic app PNGs under `brending/screenshots-real/`. Each screen must be checked at compact/medium/expanded sizes and dark/light theme. See `docs/SCREEN_SPEC.md`.
