Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Overview

SubliX is designed to manage subscription records on your Android device without requiring registration or a cloud account. This informational website does not collect subscription records.

Android app data

Subscription names, prices, categories, renewal dates and settings are stored locally on the device by the app. The app does not access banking accounts, email inboxes, SMS messages or third-party service credentials. Free and Pro use separate application data storage.

Permissions

The app may request notification permission for renewal reminders. It is not required for core recordkeeping.

Backups

Future backup features will require you to explicitly choose export or import. Avoid placing sensitive exports in publicly shared locations.

Website

This static website uses no accounts, forms, embedded tracking SDKs or advertising cookies. Your hosting provider may process standard access logs; review that provider's own policy for details. The optional Google Fonts CDN may receive normal requests when loading web fonts. GitHub links open a third-party service subject to its privacy terms.

Contact

Publisher: BRENDIGO · brendigo.com. For enquiries, use the contact information on the publisher website.

This policy must be reviewed against the final shipping app and hosting configuration before production publication.
