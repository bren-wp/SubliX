Purpose of SubliX

SubliX is a personal recordkeeping and planning application. Its calculations are estimates based on information you provide. It does not initiate payments, cancel contracts, verify invoices with providers, provide investment advice or guarantee savings.

Separate Android editions

SubliX Free and SubliX Pro are independent applications with separate Android identifiers, local databases and optional export/import for manual migration. Installing Pro does not automatically migrate data or enable an online account. Pro APK and source files published on a public GitHub Release are publicly downloadable; any future commercial distribution model would have to state separate purchase terms.

Reliability of reminders and projections

Notifications may be delayed by Android power management or device settings. Cost forecasts, budget comparisons and hypothetical savings do not incorporate every fee, tax, contract term or provider price change. Always verify with your subscription provider before taking action.

Your own exports

JSON backups and CSV reports created by the Android app are currently unencrypted. You control the file destinations and are responsible for protecting and removing sensitive exports. Import files only from sources you trust.
